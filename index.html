What your variant has, and doesn't

Based on the equipment table in Škoda India's current Kylaq brochure (the edition that includes the Sportline). Škoda notes that features can change with build date, so the brochure for your car's build month is the final word.

Factory-fitted on Classic+ AT

  • Electric sunroof with anti-pinch
  • 16-inch steel wheels with full covers, 205/60 R16 tyres; spare is a 15-inch steel wheel with a 195/65 R15 tyre
  • Climatronic automatic climate control
  • Cruise control, 2-spoke multifunction steering wheel
  • Four speakers plus two front tweeters, but no factory head unit
  • Rear parking sensors, rear defogger
  • Auto-dimming IRVM; electric ORVMs with turn indicators
  • Analog cluster with a 3.5-inch TFT display
  • LED headlamps, LED DRLs, LED tail lamps, manual coming-home lights
  • Four power windows; driver's window one-touch with pinch guard
  • Remote central locking, speed-sensing auto lock, immobiliser
  • 6 airbags with front-passenger airbag deactivation
  • Seat-belt reminder for all seats, front pretensioners, 3-point belts for all
  • ESC, TCS, ABS, EBD, multi-collision brake, electronic diff lock, brake disc wiping, rollover protection
  • ISOFIX on rear outer seats, adjustable headrests on all seats
  • Tilt and reach steering, driver seat height adjust, fabric seats
  • Front and rear LED reading lamps, 12V socket, front seatback pockets
  • Engine start-stop, listed as "Start Stop Recuperation"
  • Emergency triangle in the boot, manual handbrake lever

Not fitted from the factory (don't test)

  • Touchscreen, radio, Bluetooth, reverse camera (you're buying these from the dealer, and they get their own section below)
  • Tyre pressure monitoring (TPMS), hill-hold control
  • Paddle shifters, keyless entry and push-button start
  • Auto-folding ORVMs, automatic headlamps
  • Rear wiper and washer, rear AC vents, USB-C sockets
  • 60:40 rear seat split, front and rear centre armrests
  • Glovebox cooling, boot lamp, parcel tray, vanity mirror
  • Anti-theft alarm, dual-tone horn, fog lamps, alloys, virtual cockpit

Confirm with the dealer

  • Rain-sensing wipers: the brochure's equipment table shows them from Signature up, but launch reports and a Classic+ AT owner say Classic+ has them. Check on the car.
  • Rear-seat top-tether anchors: not listed in the brochure. Check the owner's manual.
  • Manual (+/−) selector gate: the brochure calls the gearbox "Tiptronic". Check whether the selector has a manual gate.

Before you go

  • Do the PDI before full payment and registration. Once the car is registered in your name, your leverage to reject it is mostly gone.
  • Go in daylight and insist the car is washed and dry. Mud and dust hide scratches and repaint marks.
  • Ask the dealer in advance not to start or drive the car before you arrive, so you can check a true cold start.
  • Ask for the dealer-installed infotainment and camera to be fitted before the PDI, so you can test them and their wiring at the same time.
  • Carry: a bright torch, phone camera, tyre pressure gauge, a small probe thermometer for AC vents, a bottle of water, a cloth, a phone with a music file and an Android Auto or CarPlay cable, and this sheet. Optional: a paint-thickness gauge and a VAG-compatible OBD scanner.
  • Keep the invoice, the accessory bill and the infotainment quotation with you.
