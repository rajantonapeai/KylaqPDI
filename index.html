What your variant has, and doesn't

Confirmed on Classic+ AT

  • Single-pane electric sunroof
  • 16-inch steel wheels with silver wheel caps
  • Cruise control, steering-mounted audio buttons
  • 4 speakers wired, but no head unit
  • Rain-sensing wipers, auto-dimming IRVM
  • Auto climate control, powered ORVMs with indicators
  • LED headlamps with LED DRLs, LED tail lamps
  • Tilt and telescopic steering, driver seat height adjust
  • 6 airbags, ESC, ABS with EBD, ISOFIX, 3-point belts for all seats
  • Speed-sensing automatic door locking
  • Remote central locking, 4 power windows

Not on Classic+ (don't expect these)

  • Touchscreen or any music system
  • Reverse camera (starts from Signature+)
  • Alloy wheels, front fog lamps
  • Powered or ventilated seats
  • Front armrest and parcel tray, per an owner of this exact variant

Confirm with the dealer

  • Rear wiper and washer (added to Signature in 2026; Classic+ status unclear)
  • Rear defogger, rear parking sensors, TPMS
  • Paddle shifters, hill-hold, rear AC vents working
  • Sunroof anti-pinch, 60:40 rear seat split

Before you go

  • Do the PDI before full payment and registration. Once the car is registered in your name, your leverage to reject it is mostly gone.
  • Go in daylight and insist the car is washed and dry. Mud and dust hide scratches and repaint marks.
  • Ask the dealer not to start the car before you arrive, so you can check a true cold start.
  • Carry: a bright torch, phone camera, tyre pressure gauge, a small kitchen or probe thermometer for AC vents, a bottle of water, a cloth, and this sheet. Optional: a paint-thickness gauge and a VAG-compatible OBD scanner to read stored fault codes.
  • If you find paint damage, don't accept a dealer body-shop repaint. Ask for a different car, or at least a factory panel from another car of the same colour.
